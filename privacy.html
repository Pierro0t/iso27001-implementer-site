Last updated: 23 July 2026Dernière mise à jour : 23 juillet 2026

ISO 27001 Implementer is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (answers, mistakes, exam scores, journey progress and preferences such as language) is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or using "Reset progress" in Settings, erases it.

No tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Purchases

The Premium unlock is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive or store your payment details; receipts are managed by the stores. Their handling of your data is governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for professionals and adults preparing for certification and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For any privacy question, write to pidotis+ISO27001@gmail.com.

Enfants

Modifications
